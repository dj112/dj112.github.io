Allcorrect
Sentiment report generator
Step 01

Load the workbook

The parser scans every sheet, prefers individual review rows when available, and falls back to the Region / Language sentiment table. Every row is kept.

Choose an .xlsx file or drag it here
Step 02

Review auto-filled report details

Title, platform, counts and the fetch window start are read from the workbook. Publisher comes from the workbook when present, otherwise from the built-in list or from what you entered last time for the same game. Publisher and both fetch-window dates are required. "To" always starts as today.

Step 03 (optional)

All-review table: regions and languages

Paste the total review counts straight from a spreadsheet or export. Tables with Region, Language, Reviews and Supported columns are read automatically, along with metadata lines (Game, App ID, Fetched, Source), a Total row and a second region summary. Simple lines such as "English, 1724" also work. Shares are always recalculated from the counts. This box drives the region chart, the language distribution, the coverage tiers and the related takeaways.

The on-screen report always includes the full reviews section when review rows exist. Use "Content" above to choose whether the PDF or saved HTML includes the source reviews.
