Step 02

Review auto-filled report details

Title, platform, publisher/studio and counts are filled only when the workbook exposes them. Publisher and both fetch-window dates are required; pick the dates from the calendar. “To” always starts as today.

Step 03 · optional

All-review language table

Paste one language per line, e.g. “English (EN): 1724 (30.65%)” or “English, 1724”. Percentages are optional and recalculated from the counts. This box is independent of the workbook: it is never auto-filled, and it drives only the Aggregate distribution section at the end of the report, printed exactly as entered. Use the scope field to say which product(s) and period it covers.

The on-screen report always includes the full reviews section when review rows exist. Use “PDF content” above to print the report on its own or include the source reviews.
